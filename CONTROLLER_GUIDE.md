# Controller Guide — Football Simulator with Accessibility Mod

This guide covers every control in the game and describes what the accessibility mod announces at each stage. An Xbox One or PS4/PS5 controller is required.

---

## Button Name Reference

The game uses PlayStation naming in its menus. This guide lists both.

| PS Name | Xbox Name | Description |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Cross | A | Bottom face button |
| Circle | B | Right face button |
| Square | X | Left face button |
| Triangle | Y | Top face button |
| L1 | LB | Left Bumper (shoulder button) |
| R1 | RB | Right Bumper (shoulder button) |
| L2 | LT | Left Trigger |
| R2 | RT | Right Trigger |

---

## Menus and Navigation

All menu screens are read aloud automatically. You do not need to do anything special — just navigate and the mod speaks the focused item.

| Action | Button |
|--------|--------|
| Move focus | D-pad or Left Stick |
| Select | A / Cross |
| Back | B / Circle |

**What the mod announces:** The label and type of the focused item (e.g., "Exhibition, button" or "Season, button"). When a new screen loads, the first item is announced automatically.

## Playbook — Selecting Your Play

The play call screen has two levels: formation selection and play selection.

### Formation Selection (first level)

| Action | Button |
|--------|--------|
| Browse formations | D-pad left / right |
| Confirm formation and enter play list | D-pad down or A |
| Back to previous screen | B / Circle |

**What the mod announces:** Down and distance, followed by "Select formation." and the current formation name (e.g., "1st and 10. Select formation. Shotgun.").

### Play Selection (second level)

Three plays are shown across the screen. Use these buttons to select one:

| Button | Play Slot |
|--------|-----------|
| X / Square | Left play |
| A / Cross | Middle play |
| Y / Triangle | Right play |

Use D-pad left / right to navigate to a different page of plays within the formation.

**What the mod announces:**
- When the play list opens: all three play names on the current page
- As you browse pages: the three plays on the new page
- When you confirm a play: the name of the play you selected

## Pre-Snap — At the Line of Scrimmage

After the play is called, your team walks to the line. This is when you can hear route assignments and change the play.

| Action | Button |
|--------|--------|
| Snap the ball | A / Cross |
| View play art and re-hear routes | L1 / LB |
| Open audibles | Y / Triangle |

### Route Announcement

When your team lines up, the mod automatically announces each receiver's route:

> "Routes. WR1: Slant Route. WR2: Go Route. TE: Short Out. HB: Lead Block. FB: Block."

Press **L1 / LB** at any point while at the line to hear the routes again. This maps to the game's existing "Look at play" function, which also displays the route lines on screen.

### Audibles — Changing the Play at the Line

Open the audibles screen at the line to choose a different play from three options. The mod announces them when the screen opens:

> "Audibles. X: HB Toss. A: FB Trap. B: PA Boot."

| Button | Audible |
|--------|---------|
| X / Square | Left audible |
| A / Cross | Middle / current play |
| B / Circle | Right audible |

After you accept an audible, the routes for the new play are announced automatically without needing to press L1.

## Passing — QB with the Ball

When you take the snap on a pass play and drop back, the mod announces only the **button names of open receivers**:

> "A. X."

This means the receiver assigned to A and the receiver assigned to X are open — press that button to throw. Covered receivers are not announced. If nobody is open at the snap, nothing is spoken; the mod will announce each button name as that receiver comes open during the play.

"Open" means more than 3.5 yards of separation from the nearest defender.

### Throwing to a Receiver

Each of the five receivers is assigned to one of these buttons for the current play. The game shows this visually as floating button icons over each player.

| Button | Throws to |
|--------|-----------|
| A / Cross | Assigned receiver |
| B / Circle | Assigned receiver |
| X / Square | Assigned receiver |
| Y / Triangle | Assigned receiver |
| L1 / LB | Assigned receiver |

The specific button-to-receiver assignment changes per play based on the play design.

### Other QB Actions

| Action | Button |
|--------|--------|
| Scramble (leave the pocket and run) | R1 / RB |

## Defense / Without the Ball

| Action | Button |
|--------|--------|
| Dive Tackle (aim with Left Stick) | X / Square |
| Jump | Y / Triangle |
| Strafe / Box Out (keep ball carrier in front of you) | L1 / LB |
| Switch controlled player | B / Circle |

**Tip:** Hold L1 when pursuing the ball carrier to strafe and keep them in front of you. When making a dive tackle, aim with the Left Stick before pressing X.

**Fumble tip:** If your hand or helmet hits the ball during a dive tackle, you will cause a fumble.

## On-Demand Status Readout

Press the **right stick click (R3)** at any time during the game to hear a full status summary:

> "Second quarter. 1:45 remaining. Eagles 14, Cowboys 7. 3rd and 8. Eagles 2, Cowboys 1 timeouts."

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| R3 (right stick click) | Full readout: quarter, time, score, down and distance, timeouts |
| A (keyboard) | Time remaining only |
| S (keyboard) | Score only |

## Receiver Positions — Reference

When the mod announces receiver status, it uses these position names:

| Position | Full Name | Typical Role |
|----------|-----------|--------------|
| WR1 | Wide Receiver 1 (Split End) | Outside receiver, runs deep and intermediate routes |
| WR2 | Wide Receiver 2 (Flanker) | Second outside receiver, runs routes from the other side or slot |
| TE | Tight End | Lines up beside the offensive line; runs short-to-intermediate routes and blocks |
| HB | Halfback | Running back; primary ball carrier on runs, catches short routes out of the backfield |
| FB | Fullback | Lead blocker; occasionally runs short flat or flare routes |

### Understanding Route Names

When routes are announced at pre-snap, you may hear terms like:

| Route Name | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Go Route / Fly | Straight line deep down the field |
| Slant | Cuts diagonally across the middle at a short depth |
| Post | Goes deep then cuts toward the middle |
| Corner | Goes deep then cuts toward the sideline |
| Out | Runs to a set depth then cuts toward the sideline |
| In / Dig | Runs to a set depth then cuts toward the middle |
| Curl | Runs out then turns back toward the QB |
| Screen | Short route behind the line with blockers |
| Lead Block | Runs ahead of the ball carrier to block (no pass route) |
| Block | Stays to block (no pass route) |

## Strategy Tips for Blind Play

**Before the snap**
- Always press **L1 / LB** to hear your routes before snapping. Know what each receiver is running before the ball is in the air.
- If the announced routes don't match what you want, consider calling an audible.

**When passing**
- Listen for the open/covered announcement the moment you drop back.
- If multiple receivers are open, throw to the one whose route gives you the best match-up (e.g., a deep route if you want a big gain, a short route if you need a quick release).
- If a receiver runs a blocking route (Lead Block, Block), that button will not produce a useful throw — focus on the receivers running actual pass routes.

**Audibles**
- If the defense shows a look you don't like, open audibles to switch to a run or a different pass concept.
- After accepting an audible, the new routes are announced automatically — you don't need to press L1 again.

**Running plays**
- On runs, the receiver open/covered announcement does not fire — it only triggers on pass plays when the QB drops back.
- Use B / Circle early when contact is made to churn for extra yards rather than going down immediately.
